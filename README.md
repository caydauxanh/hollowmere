# Hollowmere — a cozy valley below Ashenvale Academy

A 3D life-sim with a magic school in it. Grow herbs, brew potions, help villagers, attend lessons, get sorted into an Order, learn spells, fly a broom, fish, relight the valley's lost fires. Runs in the browser (Godot 4 web export) on PC, Mac and iPhone from one link; progress saves in the browser.

## Controls
- Move: WASD / arrows, or the joystick (touch). Drag the right half of the screen to look. Mouse wheel / pinch to zoom.
- F or ACT: talk, harvest, read, open, brew. Shift: run. Space: jump.
- 1–4: spells (Kindle, Verdant, Glimmer, Mend). B: broom (UP/DOWN buttons while flying). M: map. T: fast-forward time. Esc: close panels.
- Top buttons: HQ/LQ graphics, Map, ♪ sound, Time +6h, Villagers, Journal.

## The valley
- **Hollowmere Square** – shrine candle, notice board (requests, letters, Gazette), your apothecary garden and cauldron, your cottage (bed, hearth, trophy shelf, cat, furniture), post owl.
- **Hearth Row** (W) – the villagers' homes. **Lantern Market** (E) – Tamsin (buy herbs, sell potions and fish, garden expansions), Gorrin (broom, staff crystals), decor and cottage furniture stall. **Festival Green** (S) – maypole, bonfire, lily pond, Spark Targets, Broom Course, fireworks at 20:00, Lantern Walk on Sundays, the Star Stone. **Trial Field** (NW) – daily braziers and beds for Marks, Duelling Club 14–16.
- **Fernmoor** (SW, over the crooked bridge) – Hatch. **Ashford** (NE) – Nell and six sheep. **Lake Mirren** – fishing dock, Hatch's boat to **Heron Isle** (Old Marrow). **The Old Watchtower** (NE hill). **The Hollow Wood** (NW) – Nightcap, graves, Wennet the Grey at night.
- **Ashenvale Academy** (N, gate opens at Ember 12) – Great Hall (talking portraits, floating candles), Hall of Flame, Greenhouse, Library, Restricted Shelves, Common Room (Order beds, Order board), Potions Dungeon (+ hidden cellar), Astronomy Tower, Headmistress's Study.

## A day
Lessons: Flamecraft 8–11, Herbology 11–14, Lore 14–17, Assembly 17–19, Potions 19–21, Astronomy 21–24. Each has a 3-question quiz and a practical. Homework from one teacher a day; an Order task from your Order's student. Market 8–18, Duelling 14–16, fireworks 20:00. Villagers walk their own schedules and sleep after 21:30. Sleep in any bed for the day's ledger and a new morning.

## Story
Seven chapters: open the gate → light the dark lanterns → Quill's catalogue → the three constellations → the Watchtower → Heron Isle → the small star. Plus: the ring of fires, the unopened letter, the unlabelled bottle, the Restricted Shelves, sixteen villager memories, thirteen Founders' Cards, eight star shards, ten sky lanterns, eight things to Mend.
